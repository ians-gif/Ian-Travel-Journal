Japan 🇯🇵

Beautiful memories from Tokyo and Kyoto

Japan is a top tourist destination offering a blend of ancient tradition and modern innovation, with popular attractions including the vibrant cities of Tokyo and Osaka, historic Kyoto, and natural wonders like Mount Fuji. Visitors can experience cultural elements like the tea ceremony, and the onsen (hot springs), alongside modern conveniences such as the high-speed Shinkansen bullet train. My personal favourite? The giant anime-themed buildings selling thousands of gashapon!
